How I build

01

Bound the model

Use deterministic validation and policy checks around probabilistic components.

02

Fail explicitly

Refuse, abstain, or block when the system cannot safely justify an action.

03

Keep the boundary honest

Application-level controls are not OS isolation; a prototype is not production infrastructure.

04

Prefer evidence

Tests and documented evaluation matter more than impressive architecture diagrams.
